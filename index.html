FAQs

Before we get started.

Is domain and hosting included?

Not by default. Domain and hosting depend on the provider and plan. Recurring costs are listed separately unless included in the quote.

Does a WhatsApp button automatically confirm appointments?

No. A click-to-chat button opens WhatsApp with a prepared message. Automated confirmations, reminders or chatbot workflows need additional WhatsApp Business Platform/API setup and may have provider and message fees.

Can you build a hospital or clinic website?

Yes. Scope may include doctors, departments, facilities, visiting hours, maps, enquiry forms and appointment requests. Patient-data collection and integrations need appropriate privacy and security safeguards.

How long does a website take?

A starter site may take around 5–7 working days and a business/clinic site around 7–14 working days once content and requirements are ready. Custom projects need a separate estimate.

Do you guarantee Google rankings or leads?

No. SEO foundations and clear enquiry paths can help, but rankings and lead volume depend on many factors and cannot be guaranteed.
